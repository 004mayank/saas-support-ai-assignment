The Retention Brief Agent accepts one account object containing billing, product usage, and past-ticket records. In production those sources could come from read-only connectors; this submission deliberately uses pasted mock data, so the Agent needs no external tools and cannot write anywhere.

The Northstar Labs example shows a late invoice, weekly active users falling from forty-two to seventeen, automation volume falling by more than half, and an SSO ticket reopened twice with low customer satisfaction. The Agent reconciles those observations into a one-page brief, then separates them from hypotheses. Sponsor loss and SSO friction are possible explanations, not asserted facts. The call plan asks the retention owner to confirm the new sponsor, identify which teams stopped using the product, and agree on an access recovery and adoption checkpoint. Memory is disabled and no discount or refund is proposed.
