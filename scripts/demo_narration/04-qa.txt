The second Skill grades closed support tickets against a weighted rubric. Resolution is worth thirty points, accuracy and policy twenty-five, communication twenty, ownership fifteen, and process records ten. The example agent promises an engineering fix by tomorrow and a two-hundred-dollar refund, then hands the case off without an owner or diagnostic request.

The result is forty-one out of one hundred and fails standard. Every deduction cites visible transcript evidence. The commercial-claim cap is applied because the agent invented a refund and a binding timeline. The coaching action is specific: collect the import job identifier and logs, avoid unsupported promises, and retain ownership through escalation. A validator checks score ranges, cap logic, arithmetic, and evidence presence.
