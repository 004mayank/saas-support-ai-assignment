# Northstar Labs - retention brief

## Account snapshot
- Growth plan; USD 17,880 ARR; renewal on 30 September 2026.
- Risk flag followed the departure of the customer sponsor.

## Why the account is at risk
- Weekly active users fell from 42 to 17 between 30 June and 24 August (usage records).
- Automation runs fell from 1,280 to 410 over the same period (usage records).
- The latest invoice was paid 12 days late on 1 August (INV-883).
- The SSO ticket was reopened twice and received 2/5 CSAT (SUP-991, 12 August).

## Evidence from billing, usage, and support
- Billing: payment is current, but the most recent invoice was late.
- Usage: both active users and automation volume declined by more than half.
- Support: recent SSO friction affected access; an earlier export issue closed without a reopen.

## Retention hypotheses
- Sponsor loss may have reduced internal ownership; confirm rather than assume.
- Repeated SSO lockouts may be suppressing adoption.
- The late invoice may indicate process friction or lower commitment.

## Call plan
- Confirm the new executive and operational owners.
- Ask which teams stopped using automations and why.
- Agree on an SSO recovery owner and adoption checkpoint before renewal.

## Open questions
- Was the usage decline caused by SSO access, seasonality, or reduced business need?
- Who now owns the renewal decision?

## Confidence
- Medium-high: all three source sections are present; motivations remain hypotheses for the call.
