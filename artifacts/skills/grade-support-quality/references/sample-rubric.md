# Acme Cloud support QA rubric

Total: 100 points

| Dimension | Points | Full-credit standard |
|---|---:|---|
| Resolution | 30 | Correctly resolves the stated issue or clearly advances it with an owned next step. |
| Accuracy and policy | 25 | Makes no unsupported product, policy, pricing, refund, or timeline claim. |
| Communication | 20 | Clear, concise, empathetic, and consistent with the tone guide. |
| Ownership | 15 | Names the owner and next action; avoids unnecessary hand-offs. |
| Process and records | 10 | Captures material troubleshooting, identifiers, and disposition. |

## Caps

- Any invented refund, credit, price, or binding timeline caps the total at 49.
- Exposing credentials or sensitive personal data is an automatic score of 0.

## Ratings

- 90-100: Excellent
- 75-89: Meets standard
- 60-74: Coaching needed
- 0-59: Fails standard
