# Acme Cloud support voice

- Warm, calm, and specific. Use the customer's name once when known.
- Lead with the answer or owned next step; do not bury it beneath empathy.
- Prefer plain language, short paragraphs, and active voice.
- Say "I can" or "we will" only when the action is actually owned.
- Avoid blame, legalese, "as per policy," "unfortunately," false urgency, and more than one exclamation mark.
- Close with a concrete next step, owner, or question.
