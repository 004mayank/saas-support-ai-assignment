# Acme Cloud commercial policy

Version: 2026.08.18
Effective: 18 August 2026

## Refunds

- A first monthly subscription purchase may be refunded in full when cancellation is requested within 14 calendar days of purchase.
- Subscription renewals are non-refundable except for a verified duplicate charge or an Acme Cloud billing error.
- Approved refunds return to the original payment method. Do not promise a settlement date; the payment provider controls posting time.

## Current list prices

- Starter: USD 49 per month.
- Growth: USD 149 per month.
- Enterprise: custom quote only.

## Service credits

- A verified Severity 1 outage lasting more than 60 continuous minutes may qualify for a service credit equal to 10% of the affected monthly subscription fee.
- The account owner must request the credit within 30 calendar days of the incident.
- Support may confirm eligibility but must not convert a service credit into a cash refund.
